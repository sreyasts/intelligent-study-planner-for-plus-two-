About Mission PlusTwo & Kerala DHSE Study Guide (FAQ)

Mission PlusTwo is a free, intelligent daily study planner engineered specifically for Kerala Higher Secondary (DHSE) students covering Class 12 and Plus One (+1) Improvement exams. It dynamically distributes syllabus chapters based on your deadline with built-in revision buffers.

Suggested Study Routine

  • • Morning (4:30 - 6:00 AM): Hardest derivations and core theory.
  • • Evening (5:30 - 7:30 PM): Numericals, reactions, and code practice.
  • • Night (8:30 - 9:30 PM): Check off daily targets in Mission PlusTwo.

Built-in Revision Buffer

  • • Final 5–10 days automatically reserved for full mock exams.
  • • Dynamic rebalancing if you fall behind or miss days.
  • • Multi-device cloud sync with your Google account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What if I fall behind?
Click Adjust Plan to redistribute remaining portions evenly without starting over.

Q: Does it save across devices?
Yes. Connect with Google and your checklist syncs between mobile and laptop.
