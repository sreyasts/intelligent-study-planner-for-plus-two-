## 2. One-Page Pitch for Kerala Higher Secondary Teachers & IT Coordinators

**Subject: Free Open-Source Daily Study Planner for DHSE Class 12 (+2) and +1 Improvement Students**

Dear Respected Teachers & Coordinators,

As Kerala Higher Secondary students prepare for their First Terminal, Half-Yearly (Christmas), and March Board Examinations, many struggle to balance daily study hours across Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Computer Science / Biology.

To support students across government and aided schools, we have developed **Mission PlusTwo** (available free at [https://sreyasts.github.io/intelligent-study-planner-for-plus-two-/](https://sreyasts.github.io/intelligent-study-planner-for-plus-two-/)):

1. **Strictly Aligned with Kerala SCERT / DHSE Scheme of Work**:
   - Only current rationalized chapters (e.g. 10 Chemistry chapters, 14 Physics chapters).
   - Handles both Plus Two regular portions and simultaneous Plus One improvement revision.
2. **Dynamic Catch-Up Mechanism**:
   - If a student falls behind due to school practicals or illness, a single tap on *"Adjust Plan"* recalculates the remaining portions evenly across available days without requiring them to abandon their schedule.
3. **100% Free, Privacy-Respecting, and Ad-Free**:
   - Works completely in guest mode with zero sign-in required.
   - Built under open-source MIT License with zero commercial monetization or student tracking.
   - Fully accessible offline on mobile phones.

We welcome teachers to test the planner and share the link with their class WhatsApp groups if you find it beneficial for students.

Warm regards,  
**Sreyas T S & Mission PlusTwo Open Source Team**
