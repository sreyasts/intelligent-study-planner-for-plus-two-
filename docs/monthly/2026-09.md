## 1. Product Engineering Highlights

### Major Improvements
- **Deterministic Constraint-Propagation Engine**: Re-engineered core scheduler in `src/engine/planner.js` to eliminate heuristic instability, enforce strict chronological prerequisites, reserve mock revision buffers, and dynamically re-balance pending tasks on missed days.
- **Empirical Benchmarking Framework**: Built `scripts/benchmark-planner.js` to evaluate coverage, duplicate rates, order violations, and blank days across deterministic baseline schedulers.
- **Privacy-First Analytics Engine**: Implemented `src/analytics/tracker.js` strictly compliant with India DPDP Act and GDPR, transmitting zero PII while tracking anonymous product funnels.
- **Offline PWA & Canonical Unification**: Consolidated multi-domain traffic (`github.io` mirror) to `https://mission-plustwo.web.app/` with transparent local state migration.

### Important Bugs Fixed
- **Runway Underflow Guard**: Fixed divide-by-zero when students selected same-day or past target deadlines (`totalDaysCount <= 1`), now throwing explicit descriptive errors.
- **Duplicate Chapter Input Sanitization**: Added pre-pass deduplication filtering redundant chapter inputs.
- **Weight Clamping**: Clamped syllabus chapter weights and difficulty scores to valid numeric ranges \([1, 5]\).

## 5. Lessons Learned & Next Priorities

### What Worked
- Replacing vague "AI" claims with a verified mathematical formulation and empirical benchmarks significantly increased technical credibility.
- Local-first IndexedDB storage ensured students on spotty Kerala 4G connections never lose their schedules.

### Challenges & Gaps
- Zero external open-source contributors: The project requires clearer contributor guides, issue forms, and `good first issue` markers to attract regional student developers.
- Organic search footprint is currently limited to 5 indexed pages; expanding high-intent landing pages is critical for organic student discovery.

### Next Priorities (October 2026)
1. Launch dedicated high-intent landing pages (`/plus-two-timetable`, `/plus-one-improvement-planner`, `/study-hours-calculator`).
2. Release comprehensive contributor system with GitHub Issue Templates and Contributor Covenant 2.1.
3. Enhance Malayalam translation dictionaries with community-guided terminology.
4. Establish peer-to-peer timetable sharing via WhatsApp and Telegram.
