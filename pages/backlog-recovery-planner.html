The 4-Step Class 12 Backlog Recovery Protocol

1 Stop the Bleeding (70/30 Rule)

Never abandon current school chapters to study backlogs. If you do, today's current portion becomes tomorrow's backlog. Dedicate 70% of your evening study to current homework and 30% (e.g. 45 mins) to 1 backlog topic.

2 Prerequisite Triage

Sequence your backlog by conceptual dependencies: complete Electrostatics before Magnetism, and Differentiation before Integrals. Non-dependent chapters (e.g. Environmental Chemistry or Communication Systems) can be completed later.

3 80/20 High-Yield Focus

When behind, do NOT read 50 textbook pages word-for-word. Study SCERT summary points, core solved illustrations, and the last 5 years' Kerala DHSE board questions for that chapter.

4 Day 1 / Day 3 Spaced Testing

After finishing a backlog topic on Day 1, take 10 minutes on Day 3 to write out the key formulas and reaction names from memory without looking at notes. This cements permanent retention.
