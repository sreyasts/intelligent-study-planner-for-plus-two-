Kerala Plus Two 3-Stage Revision Roadmap

Stage 1: Syllabus & Records Through Mid-January

Complete all chapter notes, first-round concept understanding, and complete all practical laboratory fair record books.

Stage 2: Model Exam Simulation Late January – Mid February

Take full 3-hour school model exams seriously. Identify weak units (e.g. Organic conversions, Calculus, Electromagnetism) and triage immediately.

Stage 3: 10-Day Mock Sprint Final 10 Days

Mission PlusTwo automatically locks out new theory and reserves the final 10 days for timed board mock papers, formula sheets, and quick recall.
