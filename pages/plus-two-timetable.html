Kerala DHSE Practical Exam & Lab Strategy (Jan–Feb)

40 Marks / Subject

In Kerala DHSE Class 12 Science and Vocational streams, practical evaluations carry up to 40 marks (Continuous Evaluation + External Practical Exam). Many students make the mistake of pausing all theory revision in January to work on records, or ignoring practical prep until the day before the lab exam. Here is the recommended balanced protocol:

1. Fair Record Deadline

Complete all rough-to-fair record book writing, circuit diagrams, and specimen sketches by January 15. Finishing early prevents midnight cramming when DHSE model theory exams begin.

2. High-Yield Lab Viva Prep

External examiners ask principles behind experiments. Master Ohm's law, meter bridge balance, Salt analysis group reagents, titration calculations, and Python program logic. Viva prep directly aids theory marks.

3. 45-Min Daily Timebox

Cap practical record work at 45 minutes per evening. Keep your core 3-hour study block strictly dedicated to theory problem solving and chapter completions.
