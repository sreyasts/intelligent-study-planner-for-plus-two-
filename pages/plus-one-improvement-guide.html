Crucial: Syllabus Standard for Improvement Papers

Improvement candidates write the exam based on the 2025–26 DHSE / SCERT syllabus (the academic year you attended Class 11), NOT future rationalization changes. Mission PlusTwo's improvement engine is strictly calibrated to this curriculum.

The 3-Pillar Improvement Strategy

1

Focus on High-Yield Chapters

Prioritize chapters with the highest weightage in previous board question papers (e.g. Thermodynamics, Chemical Bonding, Calculus).

2

Interleaved Study Rhythm

Never abandon +2 classes completely. Dedicate 60–90 minutes in the early morning for +1 chapters and evening for +2 school homework.

3

Finish 7 Days Before Exam

All +1 portions must be wrapped up a week prior to your exam date, reserving the final week for full previous year question papers (PYQ).
